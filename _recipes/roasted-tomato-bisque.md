---
title: Roasted Tomato Bisque
category: soup
status: draft
ingredients: |-
  Tomatoes (variety)
  Onion
  Poblano Pebber
  Garlic
  Chicken Broth
  Tomato Paste
  Olive Oil
  Heavy Cream
  Italian Seasoning
  S & P
instructions: |+
  Preheat oven to 425
  Line tray with tin foil and coat with oil
  Halve tomatoes and quarter onions
  Cut off stem of poblano and deseed
  Place on tray
  Slice top off of head of garlic and wrap in foil
  Cover all with olive oil, seasoning and S&P
  Roast for 45 to 60 minutes
  Should be soft and caramelized not burned
  Heat pot on medium heat and add oil
  Add onions and squeeze garlic out of bulb into pot
  Deglaze with spash of broth and add the tomatoes and pepper
  Add in broth until covered and stir while bringing to boil
  Mix in tomato paste 
  Lower heat and blend with immersion blender until smooth
  If too thick add more broth
  Simmer for 15 minutes
  Remove from hear and stir in heavy cream
---
