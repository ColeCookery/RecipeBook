---
title: Ham & Pea Risotto
category: pasta
status: published
image: /assets/pictures/img_8862.jpeg
ingredients: |-
  Arborio Rice
  White Onion
  Chicken Stock
  Ham
  Peas
  Butter
  Olive Oil
  Salt & Pepper
  White Wine
instructions: >
  Warm Stock in a pot

  Dice half of an onion and cube ham

  Warm a slab of butter and squirt of olive oil in a pan over medium high heat

  Cook cubed ham in pan for a few minutes

  Add in onions and cook for another few minutes

  Remove from pan and set aside

  Melt another slab of butter in pan and then toss in rice

  Toast rice for a few minutes

  Deglaze with white wine

  Add in a few spoonfuls of stock to the rice and stir occasionally making sure nothing sticks to the bottom 

  When it starts to get dry repeat process

  After stock is used up and rice is no longer hard or crunchy add ham and onions back in and mix

  Lower heat and add frozen peas in and cook for the final few minutes

  Top with cracked black pepper and red pepper flakes
notes: "Add in parmesan for a cheesy flavor and serve with crunchy toasted bread "
